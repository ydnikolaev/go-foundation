## Context
R-4 set the floor at 1.25.0 from the lowest active consumer's go line (1.25.5). Measured 2026-11-08 over every go.mod on the host: that consumer imports nothing from this repository; the one importer is on 1.27.3. Go supports a release until two newer ones ship (go.dev/doc/devel/release), so 1.25 lost support when go1.27 shipped on 2026-08-19. Asked through devkit-cto with three options: keep 1.25.0, move to 1.26.0 (A), or move to 1.27.0.

## Decision
config/go-floor is the oldest supported Go release: 1.26.0 now, moved each August when a new release retires one. No unit's go line exceeds it; a unit may use go 1.26 features, never 1.27-only ones. 1.27.0 was rejected: five consumers on 1.26.x would have to bump before taking any unit.

## Consequences
units-check keeps refusing a go line above the floor. Existing units may keep a lower go line. The owner plans to move every consumer up later, which is what lets the floor follow Go's support window.
